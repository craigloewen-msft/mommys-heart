# Future brief 07: operational governance and assurance

This brief satisfies item 7 of `REQ-DOC-003`.

## User need

The organization needs operational governance for retention, legal holds,
backup/restore drills, incident response, malware scanning, accessibility,
migration/export, and independent security/compliance review before making
stronger external assurance claims.

## Scope boundary

Add operational governance for retention/legal holds, backups and restore drills,
incident response, malware scanning, accessibility, data migration/export, and
independent security/compliance review.

This brief does **not** claim those controls already exist in the MVP, nor does
it treat documentation alone as proof that they are operationally effective.

## Dependencies

- Organization-approved retention and legal-hold policy
- Backup/restore ownership and drill cadence
- Accessibility target standard and testing process
- Security review budget, scope, and remediation process

## Open policy questions

1. What retention schedule and legal-hold workflow are formally approved?
2. What restore objective and drill frequency are acceptable?
3. Which accessibility standard is the minimum release bar?
4. What independent review is required before external compliance claims?

## Acceptance outcomes

- Operational runbooks exist for retention, incident response, and restore.
- File-acceptance and malware-scanning controls are verified before attachments
  ship.
- Accessibility and independent-review expectations are explicit and testable.
