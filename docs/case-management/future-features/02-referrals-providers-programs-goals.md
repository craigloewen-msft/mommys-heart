# Future brief 02: referrals, providers, programs, and goals

This brief satisfies item 2 of `REQ-DOC-003`.

## User need

Staff need structured records for referrals, outside providers, service records,
program participation, client goals, and service-plan objectives so those facts
are not buried in free-form notes.

## Scope boundary

Add referral tracking, provider records, service records, programs, client
goals, and service-plan objectives linked to a case.

This brief does **not** define external provider portals, billing, or the full
reporting model for grants/funders.

## Dependencies

- Stable case identity and staff access controls
- Data model for external organizations and service relationships
- Longitudinal goal/objective model
- Search and audit rules consistent with note/message authorization

## Open policy questions

1. Which provider details are mandatory vs. optional?
2. Can clients see any referral or provider records in a future phase?
3. How formal must service-plan approvals and reviews be?

## Acceptance outcomes

- Staff can record referrals and outside providers without retyping them in each
  note.
- Goals and objectives can be tracked over time with visible progress state.
- Service records remain attributable and auditable.
